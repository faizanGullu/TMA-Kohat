Core Functions

What We Manage

TMA Kohat oversees a broad range of urban services ensuring orderly development, infrastructure maintenance, and regulatory compliance across the tehsil.

🏛️

Architecture & Building Control

Regulation of construction projects, issuance of building plan approvals, and enforcement of structural integrity standards across Kohat.

🚧

Enforcement & Encroachment

Removal of illegal structures, recovery of encroached public land, and GPS-logged field operations by enforcement teams.

💡

Urban Maintenance

Management of street lighting, public infrastructure upkeep, and swift resolution of civic maintenance requests.

🏘️

Housing Society Regulation

Legal action against unauthorized housing societies operating without NOCs to safeguard citizen investments in property.

📢

Illegal Advertisement Removal

Scheduled removal of unlicensed Panaflex banners and advertising charts in violation of municipal display regulations.

🤝

Inter-Government Coordination

Structured reporting pipelines and data sharing with District and Provincial Government authorities for unified governance.

Enforcement & Field Operations

Our field teams operate across Kohat with real-time logging, GPS-tagged evidence, and direct reporting to TMA headquarters.

01

Encroachment Removal

Inspectors upload GPS-tagged photos directly from the field. Each removal operation is logged with location, time, and compliance status for full accountability.

Panaflex & Ad Removal

Implementation Plan

Next Steps

The phased rollout of TMA Kohat's digital infrastructure follows a structured three-step approach.

01

Database Population

Digitizing all current records of housing societies, building approvals, lease records, and enforcement logs into a centralized, structured municipal database.

02

Portal Development

Creating a full web-based interface for the Architecture & Building Control Authority, enabling online NOC applications, complaint tracking, and public transparency dashboards.

03

Staff Training & Deployment

Orienting TMA enforcement staff, administrators, and field inspectors on digital tools, mobile reporting applications, and the new workflow management system.
